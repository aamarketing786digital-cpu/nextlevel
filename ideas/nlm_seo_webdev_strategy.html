SEO service hooks

META ADS · Cold video hook (0–3 sec) · Business owners
"Type your service + Dubai on Google right now. Did you show up? Your competitor did."
Triggers immediate action. They open their phone, search, don't find themselves, and now they're emotionally invested in your ad.
META ADS · Image ad headline · SME owners
"You've been on page 3 of Google for 2 years. That's AED 200,000 in lost business."
Quantifies the cost of inaction. Business owners respond to loss-framing more than gain-framing in the UAE market.
GOOGLE ADS · RSA Headline
"Why is your competitor ranking #1 in Dubai — and you're not?"
Question format scores high CTR on search. Implies you have the answer. Creates a knowledge gap they need to close.
META ADS · Retargeting · Website visitors who didn't convert
"Still thinking about SEO? Here's what 90 days with us looks like — real results, real Dubai clients."
Retargeting copy should acknowledge they've seen you before. "Still thinking" validates their hesitation, then overcomes it with proof.

Landing page must-have structure (every page)

1
Hero — Hook headline + sub + single CTA
Niche-specific headline. One CTA only: "Book Free Audit" or "Get Free Quote." No menu distractions. Mobile-first fold.
2
Pain section — 3 problems they recognize
Use their exact language. "Tired of paying for ads that don't work?" Mirror the pain you identified in the ad creative.
3
Social proof — Niche-matched testimonial or stat
If it's a clinic page, show a clinic result. Restaurant page = restaurant case stat. Generic proof doesn't convert.
4
What you get — 3-step simple process
"Step 1: Free audit call → Step 2: We build your strategy → Step 3: You see results." Removes confusion and fear.
5
FAQ — Overcome the top 3 objections
"How long does it take?" / "What if I've tried SEO before and it didn't work?" / "Is there a contract?" Answer these before they bounce.
6
Final CTA — WhatsApp + form
Give two options: fill a short form OR click WhatsApp. UAE users strongly prefer WhatsApp. Make it prominent.
